WHY ARE THEY EMPTY?

Reason: School data was NEVER populated!

Migration 015 creates a TRIGGER that auto-populates data when:
✅ A new school is created → Data auto-generated

But if your school was created BEFORE migration 015 applied:
❌ No trigger ran
❌ No data was created
❌ Dropdowns are empty
