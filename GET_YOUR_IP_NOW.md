## 🚀 HOW TO GET YOUR IP

### Option 1: From Your Computer Browser (EASIEST)

1. **Open browser on your computer**
2. **Go to:** `http://localhost:3000/get-ip.html`
3. **Your IP will display on the page**
4. **Copy the URL shown**

---

### Option 2: Manual Command

Open Command Prompt and type:
```
ipconfig /all
```

Look for "IPv4 Address" (like `192.168.1.105`)
