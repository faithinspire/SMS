## 🚀 EASIEST WAY TO GET YOUR IP

### Just Open This URL on Your Computer:

```
http://localhost:3000/phone-setup
```

This page will automatically show you:
- ✅ Your Computer's IP Address
- ✅ The exact URL to use on your phone
- ✅ A copy button for easy access
- ✅ Step-by-step instructions

**That's it!** No searching for ipconfig needed.

## 📱 ON YOUR PHONE

1. **Make sure phone is on the SAME WiFi as computer**
2. **Open browser** (Chrome/Safari)
3. **Go to the URL** that the setup page gives you
4. **App loads!** 🎉

## 🔄 If That Doesn't Work

### Manual Method:

1. On your computer, open browser
2. Type: `http://localhost:3000/phone-setup`
3. You'll see your IP address on the page
4. Copy it
5. On phone, go to: `http://YOUR_IP:3000`
