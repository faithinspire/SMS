WHY THIS HAPPENS:
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Supabase has TWO separate security systems:

1. DATABASE RLS
   ✅ You disabled this via migrations
   ✅ Works fine

2. STORAGE RLS
   ❌ You didn't disable this
   ❌ Blocks photo uploads
   ⚠️  NEEDS FIXING

They don't automatically sync. Each needs separate configuration.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

IS IT SAFE TO DISABLE STORAGE RLS?
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

✅ YES - For student photos

Reason: Student photos are:
- Public (visible on profiles)
- Not sensitive information
- Need public read access
- Need upload access from app

RLS is NOT needed for public photos.
It IS needed for:
- Private contracts
- Employee records
- Payroll data
- Medical information
